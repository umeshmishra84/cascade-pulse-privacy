Privacy Policy

Cascade Pulse: World Explorer · Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game “Cascade Pulse: World Explorer” (“the game”) uses and where it goes. The game is published by AUM1511 (“we”, “us”). The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

1. Information the game sends over the internet

PurposeWhat is sentWho receives it
Choosing your starting country Your device’s IP address is visible to these services when the game asks which country you are in. Only the country name is used by the game; it is not saved or sent to us. ipapi.co, ipwho.is, get.geojs.io (the game tries them in turn until one answers)
Online leaderboard The display name you type (optional, up to 14 characters; “Player” if left blank), your score, the game mode and the date. Our leaderboard database, hosted by Supabase
Live weather in each country The geographic coordinates of the country being shown (not your own location). Open-Meteo
Country flag images A standard image request, which reveals your IP address to the host. flagcdn.com

Leaderboard names and scores are visible to other players. Please do not enter your real name or any personal information as your display name.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your coins, power-ups, level progress, star ratings, settings, personal best scores and similar game data are saved on your device. This data is not sent to us.

3. Permissions

On Android the game asks only for permission to use the internet. It does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. How we use information

Leaderboard data is used only to show rankings. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or build profiles of players. The services listed in section 1 receive data because the game contacts them, and they handle it under their own policies.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a leaderboard name, contact us and we will remove it.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You can play without entering a name. To ask us to remove a leaderboard entry, email us with the name and approximate score shown on the entry. To remove locally saved game data, clear the game’s storage in your device settings or uninstall the game.

7. Security

Data sent by the game uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
